# Project Scope

## Engagement details

- **Scenario:** Mediroza General Hospital
- **Programme:** Networkwalks
- **Batch / week:** B083 / Week 4
- **Assessment:** Black-box penetration test and vulnerability assessment
- **Duration in brief:** 5 days
- **Target named in brief:** `https://medirozahospital.com`

## Stated scope and constraints

The supplied project brief describes a full black-box assessment, limited to the target domain and agreed scope. It explicitly rules out social engineering, denial-of-service testing, and testing outside scope. The brief states that written authorisation has been granted for the training exercise.

## Milestones and expected deliverables

### M1 — Initial Access
- Conduct reconnaissance within scope.
- Identify exposed entry points.
- Review authentication behaviour and input handling.
- Document proof of access and the three designated test PDF files.

### M2 — Data Extraction
- Analyse the protection/encryption on each designated file.
- Document the approved recovery approach and outcome for each file.
- Retain proof of successful access without publishing confidential contents.

### M3 — Critical Data Exposure
- Review permitted files and their properties.
- Investigate the assigned server-side exposure.
- Document the exposure involving employee salaries and shareholder details.
- Publish only redacted evidence and a non-identifying summary.

### M4 — Reporting
- Executive summary.
- Scope and methodology.
- Evidence-backed findings.
- Risk rating and justification.
- Recommendations and remediation.
